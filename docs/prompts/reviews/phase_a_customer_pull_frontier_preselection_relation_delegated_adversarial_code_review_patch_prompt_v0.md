## Fitness Reference

Goal: from a complete proposition-mode `phase_a_evidence_packet_v3`, a cold
operator can start with customer points closest to completed purchase without
discarding community-only commercial discovery, then build one bounded
thirteen-origin evidence pack whose display-eligible relations were confirmed
before selection.

Required behavior:

- account every selected product proposition exactly once across
  `retailer_first_queue`, `community_discovery_queue`, and
  `nonpromoted_points`;
- treat retailer review as first-look customer-pull venue, never an admission
  gate or universal corroboration requirement;
- retain community/qualified-audience-only customer points for explicit
  retailer check-back, while creator-authored material stays outside customer
  truth;
- retain literal venue-relative engagement without cross-platform comparison,
  prevalence, or a commercial-pull score;
- materialize exactly one admitted proposition into a v1 selection spec bound
  to the frontier, packet, bounded wording, queue, and literal semantic refs;
- use `relation_policy=bounded_point` for frontier points, so evidence of “the
  balm is expensive” supports that negative price point rather than being
  inverted by the historical positive-good-value box;
- preserve the historical `auto` value policy and v6 selected-row confirmation
  path for exact reproduction;
- before the thirteen-origin cap, independently confirm every materially
  engaged customer row, every operator-protected customer row, and every
  influence row, regardless of its first-pass relation;
- allow the confirmed relation and reason to correct the first pass, then run
  selection once over the corrected inventory;
- prohibit any selected row that did not cross confirmation;
- re-derive the embedded first-pass/confirmation replay at v7 quote
  finalization so a hand-rehashed manifest cannot claim a confirmation that did
  not occur; and
- keep repository prepare/finalize runners deterministic, no-provider, and
  no-overwrite.

The change does not claim that the 105 propositions include TikTok/creator
evidence; this exact full packet contains community and retailer roles. It does
not relabel the 7,700 unmerged semantic units, estimate customer counts, score
commercial pull, or produce a Deliver recommendation.
