## Blind success criterion (fixed now, judged after seal)

The calibrated unit is the phase-1 scout pass and its typed ledger; a
phase-2 native-return leg, if dispatched, is logged as its own unit and does
not enter the phase-1 cost share or this criterion.

The scout pass earns its mandatory seat if its fresh ledger surfaces at least
one competitor, substitute, or comparison angle that is absent from the p10
comparator layer and plausibly changes what a client would do against a named
rival — owner-judged decision-relevance, not mere name novelty. Convergence
of the fresh ledger, the retroactive ledger, and the p10 layer on the same
obvious names is evidence against the seat. This criterion may not be edited
after dispatch; a later-found criterion defect is recorded, not repaired.

## Cost-log obligation (binding on the rerun)

For the scout pass specifically, the rerun records capture counts,
block/failure counts, wall-clock time, which sub-steps ran as scripts versus
needed human or agent judgment, and any rerun step that waited on the pass.
Additionally, every commissioned unit of the rerun records `started_at` and
`ended_at`, with blocked/waiting segments typed separately from active work,
so the scout pass's cost is auditable as a share of the whole run and the run
timeline is reconstructable beside p10's existing artifact timestamps. Counts
of observed work only.

The dispatcher binds this section, the quarantine, and the fresh-pass
requirement verbatim into the rerun commission handoff; this note is not
readable by rerun actors, so the handoff is the only surface through which
these obligations reach the run.

## Decision rule

After the rerun seals, the owner reads the fresh ledger, the p10 comparator
layer, the 2026-07-28 retroactive ledger, and the cost log, then picks exactly
one — **keep** (mandatory as written), **narrow** (conditional: required only
when comparator evidence is decision-material for the bound question), or
**retire** (optional tool). Narrow and retire each need a follow-on playbook
edit as its own work unit. The adjudication is appended here with rationale
and date. This note carries no default: not adjudicating is not "keep".

If the rerun is cancelled or indefinitely deferred, the duty transfers rather
than lapses: the owner either adjudicates on the existing evidence (the
Tower 28 fresh trial and the 2026-07-28 retroactive ledger) or removes the
temporary surfaces (this note, the playbook constraint, and the scout-folder
bar) as an unfinished experiment. The silent third path — no rerun, no
ruling, surfaces lingering — is not a valid outcome.
