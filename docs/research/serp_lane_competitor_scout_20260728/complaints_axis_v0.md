## What the axis is

A complaint axis is a concrete, repeated product failing stated by
customers in their own words ("wears off in minutes", "broke me out",
"clumpy grinder"). The complaints axis is the machinery that captures,
types, corroborates, and *uses* those axes. Its central calibrated
claim: **a subject's top complaint axes predict WHERE it loses
head-to-head comparisons — not WHO beats it.**

## How the reading doctrine is calibrated (pointer)

Material doctrine changes and new evidence classes must be tested with the
cold-agent protocol in `competitor_ledger_spec_v0.md` § "Cold-agent
reading-doctrine calibration protocol." The protocol freezes an uncontaminated
expectation register before launch, uses a neutral prompt, scores load-bearing
discriminators, installs only repeatable gaps, and routes unresolved vocabulary
to the owner. This document receives complaint-axis orientation only; operative
read rules stay in the spec, so calibration cannot fork them here.

## The journey it sits in (pointer)

The chain-card IS the complaint journey: claim → buy-reason →
experience → complaint class → substitute, ending at "where the
pressure resolves." D2's five complaint classes (price-value is class
3) and the chain-card cells are owned by the choice-mechanism chain
doctrine; the competitor ledger wires into it at two points
(`competitor_ledger_spec_v0.md` § "Wiring into the claims-to-complaints
path"): complaints naming an equivalent feed the ledger inbound
(Channel 3, `complaint_body`), and finding-grade substitute entries
fill the chain-card's substitute cell outbound. Claims are the FRONT of
the same journey: a live claim under community attack (J1) is a
complaint axis being born.

## Where complaint evidence comes from (capture side)

1. **Pain-shape SERPs** — `side effects` / `not working` / `how to
   fix` / `complaints` (F6; full-bank re-judgment 2026-07-28:
   exclusive yield 0.43–0.65 on the 88-complete-subject basis, with
   `how to fix` down 0.708→0.433 — the panel-era 0.63–0.71 was
   computed on a 289/1036 partial read and is superseded; owning
   cell: serp_lane_v0.md F6). The doors, never the verdicts.
2. **Native threads** (Reddit lane) — the only composition-grade
   source. Channel 3 (complaint-borne names) is the highest-precision
   competitor channel: leaving language, not comparing language.
3. **Concern ledgers** — multi-surface, multi-year concern tracking
   per subject (the SF specimen: worth_value, burning_reaction,
   authenticity_counterfeit, each finding-grade).
4. **Rendered layers** (AIO, snippets, PAA) — the glancer's version of
   the complaints; J3 measures how faithfully they track the native
   truth; glance-value priority weights them by rendered prominence.

## Typing and corroboration (the anti-noise machinery)

- **Unmet-value map**: axes ranked by ONE-DIRECTIONAL evidence weight
  (voted complaints with zero observed defenses outrank contested
  axes), provenance-cited per quote. Contested axes are named
  contested (the T28 creasing specimen), never averaged.
- **Corroboration tiers** (owner weighting): 2 distinct authors =
  floor (easy to hit by coincidence); 3+ distinct authors = STRONG;
  3+ authors across distinct venues = STRONGEST, named when met.
  Same-author repeats collapse to one voice. One weak quote is never a
  verdict (the Bloom specimen — ground-truth confirmed). Engagement
  weighs alongside author corroboration on this side too (owner
  rule; the flag is defined in delight_axis_v0.md delta 2b): record
  each row's rendered score, apply post-as-comment where the
  statement is the post, and flag a complaint axis whose entire
  support is low-engagement ENGAGEMENT-THIN — the author tier
  stands, the confidence weighting is named.
- **Axis-occupancy read**: every top axis carries an OCCUPANTS line —
  which named products' rendered positioning sits on it — vacant vs
  occupied, with editorial vs community occupancy typed separately.
- **Axis normalization discipline**: distinct attributes are never
  joined into one axis label (falsifiability rule; the SKIN1004
  correction).
- **Speech-act posture typing (owner rule, 2026-07-28)**: every
  harvested statement is typed by its ACT before its topic, so a
  cold agent never files a question as a complaint. Postures:
  ASSERTION (first-hand experience claim — the only posture that
  feeds complaint/choice evidence directly), CLARIFICATION-SEEKING
  (doubt voiced as a question or secondhand report — markers: "I've
  seen people saying", "I heard", interrogatives, no first-hand
  failure, OP soliciting input), RESOLUTION (the thread's answer to
  a clarification-seeking row), REACTION (agreement/thanks). A
  clarification-seeking row is NEVER a complaint; it is a
  trust-pressure probe whose evidential value is its RESOLUTION —
  type that as defused / confirmed / unresolved and score the
  resolution (engagement on the question transfers to the
  resolution under the post-as-comment rule). Its engagement is
  additionally a CLARIFICATION-DEMAND signal: proof that many
  observed voters share the concern, an angle when the answer slot
  is unoccupied (operative gate and demand-signal routing:
  `competitor_ledger_spec_v0.md`, speech-act posture gate). An assertion complaint
  stays a complaint regardless of replies: defenses make it
  contested, never defused. Specimen: the Wimsylou SPF-doubt post
  (r/SkincareAddiction 1ors60c, 172 pts) — clarification-seeking,
  resolved DEFUSED (114-pt lab/Kolmar rebuttal + OP acceptance);
  scored as a defused J1 attack, not a complaint row. Counter-
  specimen: "wears off in minutes" first-hand is an assertion
  complaint even when replies defend it (contested, the T28
  creasing specimen).

## What it predicts (calibration state, 2026-07-28)

- Two independent blind runs, different model families, same 9
  subjects: complaint axes contained the comparison-deciding axis in
  **5/8 and 7/9** scorable cases (SERP-rendered verdicts); native
  settlement RAISED the signal (a rendered "miss" was a native hit —
  BoJ's sweat-resistance category exit). Live-case record 3/3
  (T28/Haus Labs on crease; SF/Laneige and SF/Ole Henriksen on
  wear-time).
- **Winner prediction is weak by design** (1/4, 2/6): complaint
  surfaces say where, comparisons say who.
- **Two named blind spots** (the boundary of the axis): the VALUE exit
  (an equivalence claim at a price gap wins regardless of the
  complaint map) and the DELIGHT axis (positive preferences complaints
  cannot see). These are not defects of the machinery; they are its
  edges, and each has its own read (value doctrine; delight-axis
  read — both in `competitor_ledger_spec_v0.md`).
- Full-bank pass (~98 subjects) re-scores everything under the four
  bound rubric rules; until then, pilot rates are pilot rates.

## Strategy the axis feeds

**For the subject/incumbent:**
1. **The top complaint axis is not a service ticket — it is the weapon
   rivals are actively winning with.** Fixing it is competitive
   defense, priced accordingly (the loss-predicts finding).
2. **Repair-room playbook** (Deliver note): when the complaint SERP
   renders repair content (RETENTION room), own/hire/preempt/alarm the
   room; when it renders comparisons (ARMED room, the CeraVe pattern),
   the response is equivalence refutation and price/size architecture,
   with a standing one-query tripwire.
3. **Early-warning**: a NEW name appearing on the subject's #1
   complaint axis is a headline alert (the Opalescence pattern) — the
   entry recipe runs through your complaint map whether you watch it
   or not.
4. **Claim hygiene (J1)**: a marketing claim under live community
   attack crossed with a pre-built cheap exit is the highest-
   consequence cell; pressure-test claims before leaning on them.

**For an entrant/challenger:**
1. **Vacant-axis entry**: enter on a vacant top complaint axis
   (Kulfi/Opalescence pattern, 3 categories observed); never enter an
   occupied axis head-on (two-front war).
2. **The ownership roadmap**: entry on the axis → explicit "Best for
   X" positioning the editorial layer can classify → unprompted
   community repetition (unfakeable; our recurrence instrument
   measures exactly this) → cross-subject citation = ownership.

**For Forseti:** the same instrument sells to both sides — the vacancy
map to challengers, the early-warning to incumbents — and the value
doctrine bounds it: we read and serve VALUE competition, never price
advice.

## Standing non-claims

Counts of observed cards/comments only, never
prevalence/volume/share; US-parameterized is not physically US-local;
rendered verdicts are never composition evidence; recurrence is not
market share; the ladder's rungs, not enthusiasm, decide what enters a
report.
