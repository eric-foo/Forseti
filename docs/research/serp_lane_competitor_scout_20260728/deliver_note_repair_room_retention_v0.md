## Method

When the exit-door read (journey lever J2) classifies a subject's
complaint SERP as a RETENTION surface — repair/usage content, zero
competitor cards — the deliverable recommendation is not defense
spending there. It is four moves that monetize the room:

1. **Own the room.** Publish official content targeting the complaint
   query itself (application technique, prep, fit tools). Google
   already ranks brand pages nearby; it will take brand content there.
2. **Hire the room.** The creators answering "try it like this" are
   doing free retention work at the moment of maximum churn intent.
   Seed/feature them before a competitor does.
3. **Preempt the room.** Move the fix upstream of the search: the
   application card in packaging and on the PDP, so the frustrated
   moment never becomes a query.
4. **Alarm the room.** Rank-watch the complaint SERP. The day repair
   content is displaced by comparison content ("{s} vs X"), the
   retention surface became an exit — a churn alarm no analytics
   dashboard fires. Cheap standing tripwire: one twin-captured query.

Framing for the client: complaints rendered as usage failures are an
asset (education gap, fixable at near-zero media cost); complaints
rendered as product/claim failures are the liability. The typing —
not the monitoring — is what allocates budget.

## The same room, seen by the attacker (offense addendum, 2026-07-28)

The playbook inverts cleanly for a client targeting the subject:

1. **Attack where they can't see it.** Unoccupied complaint queries
   (a "not working" SERP with zero competitor cards) are cheap,
   quiet intercept positions at maximum churn intent — invisible to
   the subject for months, unlike a comparative ad campaign that
   triggers legal/comms the same week. Tail-first also MEASURES
   defection demand before funding the loud frontal fight.
2. **Court the complaint creators first.** Voices already telling the
   pain story ("Made Me Breakout"; the makeup artist's return story)
   are the cheap courts: they have the audience, they already believe
   the weakness — seeding them just completes their own story.
3. **Watch the repair creators as the alarm.** They currently do the
   subject's retention for free; their content identity is built on
   making the product work, so they flip hard — but the day one posts
   a comparison instead of a fix, the subject's retention surface is
   cracking. Same signal serves defense (alarm) and offense (timing).
