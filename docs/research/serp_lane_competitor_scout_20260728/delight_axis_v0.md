## What it is

A delight axis is a positive preference that decides comparisons
without ever generating complaints when absent ("no white cast",
"one-touch operation"). Complaint surfaces are structurally blind to
it; it is one of the complaint model's two named blind spots (the
other is the value exit).

## Inherited unchanged (by pointer — do not restate, do not fork)

## The deltas (what is actually different)

1. **Source inversion.** Complaints are mined from the SUBJECT's
   surfaces; delight only exists on the RIVAL's own surfaces
   (review/reddit for the EXACT winning SKU — same-brand different-SKU
   is not the rival; first execution's zero-denominator block proved
   both points).
2. **Contamination profile.** Praise is marketing-polluted in a way
   complaints are not (#ad/#partner posts, brand-domain sourcing,
   coupon copy). Delta rules: flagged evidence never carries an axis
   alone; brand-site quotes are always flagged; paid/organic
   CONVERGENCE on one axis is itself a signal the axis is real
   (specimen: SKIN1004 advertises exactly the axes it wins with).
3. **No journey doctrine.** The complaint side has the chain-card and
   D2 behind it; delight has no owned journey analog. Named gap — do
   not invent one speculatively.
4. **Opposite strategic polarity.** A complaint axis is the subject's
   liability (fix/defend); a delight axis is the rival's asset — the
   attack that arrives as a feature nobody asked for. Consumer split:
   the Deliver note owns the playbook; this axis supplies detection.
5. **The counter-evidence store.** Mining a rival's praise surfaces
   also yields its dominant criticism — ready-made
   equivalence-breakers and shared-defect discoveries (Casabrews
   taste; OXO reliability) that the subject-side read cannot see.
