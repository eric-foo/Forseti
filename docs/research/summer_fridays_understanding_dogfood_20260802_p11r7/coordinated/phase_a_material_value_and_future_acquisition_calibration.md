## 1. Executive decision

**Recommendation: `SEAL_AFTER_OWNER_ADJUDICATION`.** No additional acquisition is necessary for the bounded qualitative competitive-intelligence purpose of this Phase A. All twelve competitive axes recompute as `strong` with distributed independent origins and choice evidence `[pack: axis_summaries[].current_strength_recomputed, .distributed, .choice_ok]`. The final four search pairs (Y/Z, AA/AB, AC/AD, AE/AF) together added 52 incrementally admitted-and-used threads, 12 deduplicated behavior events, and approximately zero new communities or new competitor labels, and none of them changed a competitive conclusion `[pack: pair_summaries[].incremental_policy_snapshot; derived]`. The final material-exhaustion test, AE/AF, ran 24 varied searches and produced exactly 2 usable threads, 6 coding rows, and 0 behavior events, both threads being ordinary corroboration of already-strong axes `[pack: pair_summaries["AE/AF"].incremental_policy_snapshot; pack: community_coding_rows for 1e57hgs, 1ep8x4l]`.

The old assembler rule — any usable Reddit thread resets the universal search requirement — is the wrong stopping model and should not govern the seal. Applied to this record it would have demanded another full universal pair after AE/AF's two materially non-novel threads, and there is no evidence-derived point at which it terminates on a large brand `[pack: phase_state.finalization_blocker.exact_failure]`. The replacement model that this analysis independently supports is an **evidence floor plus material exhaustion** rule, with the floor defined by independence structure (channels, communities, behavior evidence) rather than thread counts, and exhaustion demonstrated by two consecutive genuinely varied query families yielding no material addition. Section 10 specifies it; Section 11 replays it against this run.

This is an advisory recommendation. It is not a seal, a validation claim, a readiness finding, or permission to start Deliver. The owner must also adjudicate the assembler conflict recorded in the finalization blocker before any seal is written `[pack: phase_state.finalization_blocker.next_action]`.

## 10. Stopping and reopening rule

**Candidate models pressure-tested against this corpus:**

1. **Fixed numeric floor only** (20 / 40 / 500 threads or similar). Under-stopping and over-stopping simultaneously, depending on the number chosen: a 40-thread floor stops before E/F's behavior harvest exists (opinion-only corpus); a 500-thread floor forces continuation deep into the dry tail regardless of yield. Blind to independence and duplication — repeated captures of one community could satisfy it. On this corpus: no fixed number lands on the true saturation region (roughly post-W/X) except by luck. **Rejected.**
2. **Source exhaustion only** (the old assembler: any usable thread resets universal search). Endless-search risk is not hypothetical — it is this run's live blocker: AC's 13 usable threads forced two dry full-axis sweeps; AE/AF's 2 ordinary threads would force another `[pack: phase_state.finalization_blocker]`. On a large brand the supply of *usable* threads is effectively unbounded, so the rule never terminates on evidence; it terminates only when an owner overrides it — which is what happened. Highly susceptible to duplicated/repetitive evidence. **Rejected.**
3. **Evidence floor AND material exhaustion (conjunctive).** The floor (Section 9) prevents premature stopping while the corpus is still forming (it is unsatisfiable at A/B-stage when behavior and destination evidence are missing); material exhaustion — two consecutive genuinely varied query families, each a designed family from the vocabulary plan rather than a rewording, yielding no material addition (no new pain/delight seam, no evidence-tier change, no new segment/condition/competitor-destination/contradiction that changes a decision) — prevents endless search because ordinary corroboration and mere usability do not reset anything. Duplicated evidence cannot extend the run. Cost: up to two confirmation pairs (~2–3 h at this run's continuation cadence). On this corpus: floors satisfied for all axes by roughly U/V–W/X; exhaustion demonstrated by AC/AD + AE/AF at the latest (Section 11 explores the earlier stop under an optimized family order). **Recommended.**
4. **Axis-weighted / evidence-tiered hybrid** (per-axis weights, tier scores, weighted saturation index). Its only real advantage — per-axis reopening precision — is already inside model 3's reopening clause; the rest is standing scoring ceremony (weight maintenance, tier definitions, per-round scoring) whose recurring cost exceeds any observed benefit: on this corpus all axes matured inside a two-pair window (U/V–W/X, with formula trailing to Y/Z–AA/AB), so weighting would have changed nothing. **Rejected as standing machinery; adopt only its reopening scoping.**

**Recommended rule (model 3), fully stated:**

- **Continue** while any axis fails its structural floor, or while planned vocabulary families remain untried.
- **Stop** when every axis satisfies the floor AND the two most recent genuinely varied query families each produced no material addition.
- **Ordinary corroboration** (repetition of an established point, however usable) never resets anything; it may raise stated confidence.
- **A material addition reopens only its own axis and directly adjacent axes when justified** (adjacency = shared mechanism or shared product surface, e.g., a sour-smell instance reopens formula_consistency and justifies checking scent_and_flavor, not all 12), and reopening means targeted acquisition for that axis, not a universal pair.
- **Premature-stop protection during baseline:** the exhaustion clock does not start until the baseline (route steps 1–6) and the vocabulary plan's high-yield families have run; two dry pairs during axis formation cannot stop the run because floors are still unsatisfied.
- **Exhaustion is demonstrable without claiming the internet is empty:** the claim is "the two most recent varied designed families yielded no material addition," a bounded, evidence-backed statement about designed effort, not about the existence of further pages `[handoff: Source gaps; this rule deliberately matches that boundary]`.
- **Reopening trigger after sealing** is out of scope for acquisition but noted for the owner: a post-seal material addition (e.g., a verified reformulation event) reopens its axis under the same scoping.
