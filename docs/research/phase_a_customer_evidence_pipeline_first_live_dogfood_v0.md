source-heavy acquisition. During the dogfood, bounded fixes added:

- inspection of RealChrome access-block metadata even when its subprocess exits
  zero;

  the same run root with `BLOCKED_CONCURRENT_PIPELINE_PROCESS`.

`broad_consumer_brand_understanding_v3` / `understanding_evidence_depth_v4`
coupling. The Dieux internal commission-stage board passed that validator. The
initiating Reddit URL and advisory scan remained investigation pointers only.

runner can visibly launch or reuse the exact loopback CDP-bound Chrome profile,
proves the process/profile binding, never closes operator Chrome, and leaves a
CAPTCHA or block visible for owner handoff.
