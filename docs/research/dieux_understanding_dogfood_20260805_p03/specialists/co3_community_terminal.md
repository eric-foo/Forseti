## 4. Current community evidence

These are source-native, self-selected qualitative observations. They establish
that a behavior, condition, complaint, delight, or comparison exists inside the
captured threads; they do not establish frequency, causation, safety, or market
consensus.

### Hydration, barrier, weight, finish, and use conditions

- Instant Angel is strongly segment- and condition-dependent. Captured users describe repeat purchase, a pillowy/dewy midweight feel, tretinoin-buffer value, makeup compatibility, and barrier comfort; others describe insufficient overnight hydration, tight/dry or flaky finish, grease or tack, poor absorption, or a need to layer another hydrator or occlusive.
- Air Angel is repeatedly positioned and used as the lighter daytime, oily/combo, summer, or humid-climate choice. It can work under SPF/makeup and avoid stickiness, while dry or tretinoin-stressed users can find it too light, use it as a serum-like layer, or place Instant Angel/Skin Mercy over it.
- Skin Mercy fills a distinct soothing/recovery slot for some captured users: winter dryness, actives, redness, eczema or flare conditions, and hand use. Counterevidence includes flaky wake-up and return, immediate itching/eye sting/redness, price hesitation, and reports that it is merely adequate. Texture reports place it richer than Air Angel, less dewy than Instant Angel, and less surface-occlusive than Tatcha Indigo.
- Instant Angel's role changes by climate, season, actives, and time of day. Several users rotate Air Angel/Instant Angel or reserve richer Avene, LRP, Nivea, Skinfix, Aestura, or other creams for night or repair rather than treating one moisturizer as universally best.
- The bounded hero comparison rejects a durable wrinkle-fading inference: commenters distinguish temporary hydration/plumping and less-visible lines from actual removal of existing wrinkles.

### Reaction, smell, packaging, and trust

- Current exact-thread bodies contain first-hand reports of itching, burning, swelling, redness, dermatitis-like rash, tightness, roughness, breakouts, pustules, or perioral/rosacea flares associated by the writers with Instant Angel, Air Angel, Deliverance, Skin Mercy, or Baptism. Other commenters report no reaction, repeat purchase, or substantial calming/barrier benefit. Ingredient and preservative explanations in comments remain user hypotheses, not verified causal findings.
- The weekly candidate documents an executed return and purchase from a different store after a strong mildew/damp-cloth smell; the replacement smelled the same to that writer. The thread contains substantial counterevidence from repeat users who smell nothing or only a faint ingredient, bandage, or glue note, sometimes transient. This supports odor-perception and nonrepurchase/return behavior, not contamination or batch-failure proof.
- A Trustpilot first-hand Air Angel review reports a pump that leaves weeks of product inaccessible and manual scooping/cleaning, leading to stated nonrepurchase despite formula praise. Community bodies also value tube hygiene or reject tubs, while stockouts and small/quickly consumed formats can trigger replacement search.
- Hype, founder marketing, TikTok promotion, price, international shipping effort, stockouts, Sephora/subscriber prioritization, and brand transparency all appear as buying-confidence mediators. The same current bodies include strong founder/brand affinity and repeat purchase, so a one-direction trust conclusion is not supported.
