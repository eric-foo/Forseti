## Final kept boundary

The final ordering inside each retailer-first or community-discovery queue is:

1. more independent supporting origins;
2. when origin counts tie, cross-role independent recurrence;
3. more materially engaged supporting evidence items;
4. reported behavior as the final commercial tie-breaker; and
5. proposition identity for deterministic order.

Unavailable engagement cannot earn materiality. Material items that share an
origin do not increase independent recurrence. Operator-protected safety and
costly-behavior points remain admitted and accounted but receive no priority
solely from protection.

Packet v3, source and relation membership, retailer-first routing, the
thirteen-origin point-pack cap, provider behavior, and completed production
outputs remain unchanged.
