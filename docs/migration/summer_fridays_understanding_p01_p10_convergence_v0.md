## Boundary

This is a one-time convergence record, not a new checklist or source of live
Understanding doctrine. Historical p01-p10 artifacts remain immutable evidence
of what happened. Future runs load the three `open_next` authorities and the
current acquisition playbook/validator; they do not need to reconstruct this
iteration history.

The convergence uses these dispositions:

- `adopt_generic` — a reusable lesson is now explicit in current authority;
- `already_owned` — current authority already carried it before this pass;
- `case_specific` — useful run provenance, not a general rule;
- `superseded` — a later iteration replaced the mechanism while retaining the
  underlying goal;
- `rejected` — retaining it would recreate a demonstrated failure; and
- `process_defect` — a review, prompt, or seal shape allowed a false or fragile
  completion decision and therefore required a guard at the decision node.

## Installed Generic Rule

The observed recurring loss class is: a broad company acquisition can complete
all planned jobs and touch every named venue while still carrying only token
depth inside outside-in, community, review, or native-social families.

The causal miss was the absence of an enforced distinction between
evidence-family coverage and independent within-family depth at the acquisition
seal decision. The installed intervention is therefore bounded to that node:

> For `broad_company_understanding_v1`, a passing v3 seal requires the
> evidence-depth ledger to clear the anti-token floors and the adaptive
> saturation closure; completed job accounting alone cannot authorize Deliver.

The under-case is the Summer Fridays p11r3-shaped seal: all jobs complete, but
no depth or saturation evidence. The current validator rejects that shape as a
new broad-Understanding seal. The over-edge is a high-volume corpus with open
material seams or a final batch that still adds material value: it also remains
blocked, so numeric volume cannot manufacture closure.

## What Was Not Installed

- No mega-manual containing p01-p10 prompt prose.
- No requirement for future agents to read this migration record.
- No universal claim that the initial profile floors are statistically
  representative of a market.
- No rule that a represented source family is exhausted.
- No new review pass, task topology, registry, or recurring migration receipt.
- No modification of historical p01-p10 evidence or seals.

are not a `workflow-distill` deployment, resolver, or installed-cell claim.
